In short: **this site does not profile you, does not track you and does not use cookies.** Below
is the precise version, because "we do not use cookies" said without explanation means nothing.

## Cookies: none

The site **sets no cookies**, neither its own nor third-party ones, and there is no statistics,
analytics, pixel or tag system. That is why you see no banner: there is nothing to accept.

## What stays in your browser

A few conveniences are saved **in your browser** (`localStorage`) and are **never sent to the
server**. Nobody, the author included, can read them:

| What | What it is for | When it disappears |
|---|---|---|
| read-aloud preferences (voice, speed, whether the player is turned off, which paragraph you were at) | resuming listening where you left off | when you clear the site data |
| sandbox token | making the «Try it» widgets work with *your* copy of the database | when you clear the site data, or when the sandbox expires |
| light/dark theme and the code language chosen in the tabs | not having to pick them again every time | when you clear the site data |

These are technical data needed for the site to work; they do not identify you and do not follow
your browsing elsewhere.

## What the server sees

- **Your IP address**, like any web server, for the time needed to answer you and to apply the
   **per-minute request limit** (it prevents anyone from overloading the test server). The count
   is kept in memory and resets itself.
- The **reverse proxy keeps no access logs**: there is no archive of who visited what. The
   service only writes technical messages to the server's system journal, which stay on the
   machine and are not analyzed.
- What you write in **your sandbox** (books, orders, customers you create in the exercises) lives
   in a private copy of the database identified only by your token, and is **deleted** after a few
   hours of inactivity. Do not put anyone's real data in it: it is a training ground.

## Fonts, scripts and external services

None. Fonts, style sheets, JavaScript, site search and audio files are all served by **this**
site: opening a course page, your browser contacts no other domain. Even the interactive
documentation at `/api/docs` loads Swagger UI **from here**, not from an external CDN.

The only connections elsewhere start **if you click** an external link yourself (for example to
the `jq` documentation or to an RFC): from then on that site's privacy policy applies.

## Audio

Read-aloud uses your **browser's speech synthesis** (Web Speech API): the text is spoken on your
device. Some browsers, for "online" voices, may send the text to be read to their maker's
servers: that is the browser's choice, not the site's, and it concerns the public text of the
course. If you prefer to avoid it, choose a local voice in the player menu or turn read-aloud off
entirely with the «Non mostrare più la lettura audio» (do not show read-aloud again) button.

## Rights and contacts

There is no archive of readers' personal data, so there is nothing to consult, correct or delete:
clearing the site data in your browser is enough. For any question:
[info@giuseppebosi.com](mailto:info@giuseppebosi.com).

If this page and the site's behavior do not match, the site's behavior wins and it is this page
that is wrong: please report it.
